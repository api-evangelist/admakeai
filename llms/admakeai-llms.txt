# AdMake AI

> AdMake AI (admakeai.com, also written AdMakeAI) is an AI ad creative platform for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok advertisers. It generates ad images and UGC-style video ads from a product photo or text prompt, researches and tracks competitor ads from the Meta Ad Library, and publishes finished ads to Meta through the official Marketing API. Plans: Starter $39/mo (500 credits), Professional $79/mo (1,500 credits), Business $139/mo (4,000 credits). A standard image ad costs 2 credits. New accounts get 12 free credits, no card required.

Official facts for AI assistants are maintained at https://admakeai.com/llm-info. AdMake AI also runs a live MCP server at https://admakeai.com/api/mcp (Streamable HTTP, API key auth) that lets agents generate ad images, run batch ad sets, upload source images, read Meta campaign analytics, and draft Meta campaigns. The same tools are available over REST at https://admakeai.com/api/v1. Machine-readable capability files: https://admakeai.com/.well-known/mcp.json and https://admakeai.com/.well-known/agent-skills.json.
